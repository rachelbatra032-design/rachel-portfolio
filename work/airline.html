Airline Industry Market Entry

Rise and competition of the airline industry in India. SVNIT finals. Team The Confusement Trio.

TeamThe Confusement Trio — Mallika Mandal, Rachel Elsa Jude
CaseBrand and entry, not a livery exercise

Opening conditions

India is already one of the world’s larger domestic aviation markets. Passenger traffic and aircraft demand are climbing. Connectivity outside the metros is still thin. Airport modernisation and 100% FDI on the automatic route sit in the brief as facts, not as a slogan.

The constraints that matter

Capacity. Regional routes. A middle class that is mobile and still price-sensitive. The full recommendation is in the competition deck.
