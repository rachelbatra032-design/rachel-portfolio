Strategic Expansion Blueprint for IKEA India

The landscape

The deck maps the market by sector, supply, type, and city. Organised players — Godrej, Pepperfry, Durian — sit in a market that is still about 80% unorganised.

Demand-side constraints

Hyper-digitisation, sustainability, and personalisation are treated as constraints, not slogans. The full recommendation lives in the competition deck. This page is the archive of that brief.
