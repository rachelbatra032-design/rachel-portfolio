Growth for a Home Appliances Company

King Hardware, exploring new avenues in home appliances. IIT Kanpur. Team The Confusement Trio.

TeamThe Confusement Trio — Mallika Mandal, Rachel Elsa Jude
BriefGrowth for a line that already exists

The industry frame

Fragmented market. High capital to enter. Cheap Asian substitutes. Buyers who can switch. Smart appliances are the growth slice; most volume is still ordinary hardware.

What the case is not

It is not a greenfield brand. The question is where to push a line that is already on the shelf. The full recommendation is in the competition deck.
