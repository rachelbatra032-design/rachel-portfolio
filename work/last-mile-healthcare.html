ConstraintCare still fails at the last mile

The sector as the deck frames it

Growth is high. Public spend per person is low. Out-of-pocket costs do most of the work. The industry can expand and still leave people without a usable last mile.

The strategy problem

Insurance penetration. Low-cost chains. Tier-2 and tier-3 cities. A shift from episodic hospital care to something that can live outside it. The writeup stays at that constraint. The full recommendation lives in the competition deck.
